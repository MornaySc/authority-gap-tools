Performance and development do not fully explain advancement. Someone can be performing well and developing continuously and still not advance. The question you need to ask is what is happening between the contribution you are making and what happens next.

The Executive Authority Gap Baseline measures the structural conditions of a professional's current advancement position, revealing where their contribution is enabling movement, where it is being constrained, and which pattern is most responsible for the gap between their current position and the trajectory they want to build.

In your own words

Something is not working. You know it.

This is not the place for the polished version. Not how you would explain it to HR, not how you would frame it in a performance review. This is the place for what is actually happening, as clearly as you can say it.

Write about the specific moments that have stayed with you. The conversation that did not go the way it should have. The result that landed without your name on it. The room you were not in. Write about what you have tried, and what happened. What you think is driving it. What you want, not in abstract terms, but specifically.

Minimum 400 words. No maximum. The gaps, the frustrations, the things that do not make sense - those are exactly what is useful here.
